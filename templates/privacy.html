Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password. When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical data, such as browser type and access times, to keep the service running.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, show your expenses and summaries, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers we control, and passwords are stored in hashed form rather than plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, please contact us through the support details provided on the Spendly website.
